## Formal definition

**Tunsicelody** is a unified musical composition or melodic identity in which a memorable primary tune is integrated with interconnected supporting melodies so that the combined melodic material functions as one cohesive musical expression.

## Origin

The name **Tunsicelody** is a coined portmanteau derived from the concepts of **tune**, **music**, and **melody**.

Danvion Michael Grant created the term to distinguish the identity of an entire interacting melodic structure from a single isolated melody. The concept can encompass a principal tune together with counter-melodies, bass movement, harmonic lines, motifs, and other supporting melodic elements.
