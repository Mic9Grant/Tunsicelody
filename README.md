## Definition

**Tunsicelody** is a unified musical composition or melodic identity in which a memorable primary tune is integrated with interconnected supporting melodies so that the combined melodic material functions as one cohesive musical expression.

A tunsicelody may involve a principal vocal or instrumental melody together with counter-melodies, bass movement, harmonic lines, recurring motifs, and other supporting melodic material.

## Etymology

The term **Tunsicelody** was formed from **tune + music + melody**.
