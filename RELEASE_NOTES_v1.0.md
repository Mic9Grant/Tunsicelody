## Tunsicelody

**Pronunciation:** tun-sih-SELL-oh-dee  
**IPA:** /ˌtʌn.sɪˈsɛl.ə.di/  
**Part of speech:** noun  
**Plural:** tunsicelodies  
**Coinage year:** 2026

**Definition:** Tunsicelody is a unified musical composition or melodic identity in which a memorable primary tune is integrated with interconnected supporting melodies so that the combined melodic material functions as one cohesive musical expression.

The term was formed from **tune + music + melody** and was coined and defined by **Danvion Michael Grant**.
