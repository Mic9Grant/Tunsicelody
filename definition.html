Tunsicelody

tun-sih-SELL-oh-dee · /ˌtʌn.sɪˈsɛl.ə.di/ · noun · plural: tunsicelodies
Tunsicelody is a unified musical composition or melodic identity in which a memorable primary tune is integrated with interconnected supporting melodies so that the combined melodic material functions as one cohesive musical expression.

Expanded explanation

A tunsicelody may include a principal vocal or instrumental tune together with counter-melodies, bass movement, harmonic lines, recurring motifs, response phrases, and other supporting melodic material. The defining idea is that these elements are understood as parts of one larger melodic identity rather than as unrelated melodic fragments.

Etymology

The word Tunsicelody is a coined portmanteau formed from tune + music + melody.

Conceptual distinction

A single melody can be understood as one identifiable sequence or line. Tunsicelody refers to the larger melodic identity produced when a primary tune and its supporting melodic elements function together as a coordinated musical expression.
